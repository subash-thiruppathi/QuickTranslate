🚀 3 Quick Steps to Get Started

1
Pin QuickTranslate for quick access

Click the Extensions icon (🧩) in your Chrome toolbar, then click the Pin icon next to QuickTranslate.

2
Select any text on any webpage

Whenever you highlight text, the translation card will immediately pop up near your cursor with audio pronunciation and copy options.

3
Drag and customize anytime

Click and drag the header or grip handle (⋮⋮) to reposition the card anywhere you want. Click the extension icon to change your default language.
